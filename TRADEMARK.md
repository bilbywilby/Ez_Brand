# DevHound Trademark & Brand Assets Policy

Last Updated: 2026-08-05  
Version: 1.0.0  

## 1. Purpose
This policy governs the use of the DevHound name, logos, and visual identifiers. The objective is to ensure that community members and commercial entities can accurately reference DevHound while protecting the brand identity from dilution, unauthorized commercial exploitation, or misrepresentation.

## 2. Protected Marks
The following visual and textual assets are registered trademarks or pending marks of `@bilbywilby` / DevHound Project:

1. **Wordmarks**:
   * `DevHound`
   * `DevHound AI`
2. **Visual Identifiers**:
   * The stylized husky head mascot featuring upright ears, symmetrical forehead blaze, and black-and-white vector line art.
   * The collar featuring a triangular pendant tag containing an uppercase letter **"D"** ("D-tag mascot").
   * The combined Wordmark + Mascot logotype layout.

## 3. Allowed Uses (No Written Permission Required)

You may use DevHound wordmarks and unaltered logo assets without formal written permission in the following contexts, provided there is no implication of official sponsorship or endorsement:

* **Educational & Informational**: Personal blog posts, software reviews, tutorials, academic research, and tech conference presentations.
* **Software Compatibility**: Technical statements indicating integration or compatibility (e.g., "DevHound plugin for VS Code", "Built for DevHound pipelines").
* **Community Forks**: Referencing the original upstream source in documentation (e.g., "Forked from DevHound"). *Note: See Section 5 for logo requirements on forks.*

## 4. Prohibited Uses

The following uses are strictly prohibited without prior written authorization from `legal@devhound.app`:

* **Trademark Removal on Official Assets**: Distributing official binaries, packages, or documentation where the triangular "D" tag is intentionally stripped while keeping the DevHound project name.
* **Commercial Merchandise**: Manufacturing or selling physical merchandise (clothing, stickers, hardware badges, mugs) bearing the DevHound name or husky logo.
* **Domain Names & Company Names**: Registering domain names, legal entity names, or trademark registrations that incorporate "DevHound" or confusingly similar variations.
* **False Endorsement**: Using the logo in vendor marketing collateral, investor pitch decks, or service landing pages in a manner that implies official partnership or certified status without an active corporate agreement.

## 5. Rules for Community Forks & Derived Software

To prevent consumer confusion between community-maintained forks and upstream releases:

1. **Remove Official Trademarks**: Community forks must remove the primary DevHound logotype and modify the husky mascot to remove the "D" collar tag.
2. **Distinct Branding**: Community forks must use a distinct project name and primary accent color (differing from official `#6d4aff` purple).
3. **Attribution Statement**: Include a notice in the `README.md`:  
   `"This project is derived from DevHound (https://devhound.app). DevHound is a trademark of bilbywilby."`

## 6. Requesting Authorization
For commercial licensing inquiries, media kit requests, or sponsorship verification:

* **Contact Email**: `branding@devhound.app`
* **Response SLA**: Within 5 business days.
